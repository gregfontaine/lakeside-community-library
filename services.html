Membership

How to join

Bring photo ID and proof of address to the front desk. Membership is free for residents.

Borrowing

Up to 12 items at a time, for three weeks, with two renewals if no one else has reserved them.

Reservations

Reserve items online or at the desk; we'll email you when they're ready to collect.

On-site

Wi-Fi

Free Wi-Fi throughout the building for all visitors.

Printing & copying

10 cents per black-and-white page, 40 cents for colour. Scanning is free.

Study rooms

Two bookable study rooms seating up to six, free for members.

For younger readers

A dedicated children's corner, weekly story time, and a summer reading challenge run throughout the school holidays.
