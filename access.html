Getting here

The library sits on the lakefront, a few minutes from the town centre.

Address

14 Maple Avenue, Lakeside. The entrance is on the north side, next to the car park.

By bus

Routes 4 and 12 stop at "Lakeside Promenade", a two-minute walk away.

By bike

Covered bike racks are available at the main entrance.

Parking

Free parking for library visitors is available for up to three hours in the lot adjoining the building. Two accessible bays are located nearest the entrance.

Accessibility

The building is step-free throughout, with a lift to the first floor, accessible toilets on both levels, and a hearing loop at the front desk.
